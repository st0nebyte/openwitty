# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in OpenWitty. **OpenWitty is unofficial: not made, supported or endorsed by LEWITT.**
Please report OpenWitty problems here, not to LEWITT.

Everyone taking part agrees to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How you can help

- **Report a bug:** open an [issue](https://github.com/st0nebyte/openwitty/issues/new/choose) with what happened,
  what you expected, your distribution and desktop, and the output of `openwitty-cli diagnose` (read-only).
- **Tell us how it runs on your system:** OpenWitty was tested on one system only (KDE Plasma 6, PipeWire).
  Reports from other distributions and desktops are very welcome ("Works / does not work on my system").
- **Ideas and questions:** please use [Discussions](https://github.com/st0nebyte/openwitty/discussions).
- **Protocol findings** about the CONNECT 2 (or other CONNECT devices): open an issue or a discussion.

## Code changes

OpenWitty's code is written by its maintainers; **pull requests from outside are not merged** at the moment. If you
need a change, please open an issue or a discussion describing it; we may implement it. You are free to fork the
project under the terms of the MIT licence.

## Safety

OpenWitty writes to a real audio device. Please never post commands that change device settings as "try this"
advice in issues; changes belong in reviewed releases.
